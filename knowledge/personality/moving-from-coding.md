I'm moving away from coding-heavy QA work because I want to influence how quality and delivery work across an organization: shaping strategy, tools, and culture, not just writing tests. That is why I'm now focused on Product Owner / Associate Product Manager, Scrum Master / Agile Delivery Lead, and Associate Technical Program Manager roles. I bring 7 years of QA and delivery experience to that work, including mentoring 3 junior QA engineers, coordinating a 6-person QA team, cutting the regression cycle by 40%, and moving releases from biweekly to weekly.
