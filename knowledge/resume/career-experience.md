My career in QA automation and software development spans about seven years across four companies, from ed-tech startups to a regulatory compliance lab to Fortune 500 financial services.

I started at Quantiphi as an intern and moved to Quality Analyst (August 2018 to December 2019), then was a Product Quality Specialist II at iSchoolConnect from December 2019 to August 2022, then a QA and Automation Engineer at UL Solutions from July 2024 to June 2025, and most recently a Software Engineer in Test at Prudential Financial from July 2025 to August 2026. That is about seven years across four companies.

There is a gap between August 2022 and July 2024 that my site and resume material do not explain. I stayed longest at iSchoolConnect, about two years and eight months. Each step added scope: bigger suites, bigger teams, and more ownership of strategy and delivery.
