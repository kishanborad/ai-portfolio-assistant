From August 2018 to December 2019 I worked at Quantiphi, starting as an intern and moving to full-time Quality Analyst. It was my first QA role and the start of my career in test automation.

I scaled their Selenium WebDriver suite from zero to 120+ test cases across 4 product lines, cutting manual regression cycle time by 40%, with same-day developer feedback on every pull request.

Did I do cross-browser testing? Yes. I executed functional, unit, and cross-browser testing across Chrome, Firefox, IE, and Opera, remediating 80+ pre-launch UI defects before every production release across all four product lines.
