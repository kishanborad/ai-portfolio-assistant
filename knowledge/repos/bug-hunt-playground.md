Bug Hunt: Manual vs Automated is a playground that runs accessibility, functional, and visual probes against demo scenarios, showing what manual testing and automated probing each catch. It presents bug lists, pins, tooltips, and a report card. It is not one of the six projects featured on my portfolio site.

It runs three probe engines: accessibility probes powered by axe-core, functional probes, and visual probes. The four demo scenarios are a health form, a metric board, a shop, and a travel blog.

Bugs are presented as lists with pins and tooltips on the demo scenarios, plus a report card summarizing what the probes found.
